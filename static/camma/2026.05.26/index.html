Fix the local frame before estimating the CBD

  • Gallbladder detection is often too coarse: masks are too large and can include the CBD location.
  • Consequence: the local frame is not well defined.
  • Solution: scale the training with a larger SAM3, fine-tuned with LoRA.
  • Joint training: gallbladder on EndoScapes + IBSAFE key frames, liver on IBSAFE key frames.
  • Result: masks are much cleaner.
  • Because the model is ICG-agnostic, CBD estimation from the two masks can also stay ICG-agnostic.
